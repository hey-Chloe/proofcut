## Criteria discrepancy

The official Overview shows Functionality 30%, Creativity 20%, Technical
execution 20%, Real-world usefulness 30%. The official Rules page shows the
same criteria as 30%, 20%, 30%, 20%. ProofCut therefore treats functionality,
technical execution and usefulness as equally important quality gates instead
of relying on the lower weight shown on either page.

## Account-state boundary

At the authenticated check, the account had not joined this hackathon and had
no project draft. Joining requires last name, participation mode, referral
source and confirmations of eligibility and the rules. Those identity and legal
steps require the user's direct action/confirmation and were not performed by
the local build.
